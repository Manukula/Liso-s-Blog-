To Love Someone ❤️

To love someone to eternity, you must learn to love the good, the bad, and the ugly of the person. You must learn to love their smiles and their tears. Their happiness and their sadness. Their strengths and their weaknesses. You must learn to accept their mistakes, their fears, their insecurities, and the parts of themselves that they sometimes struggle to love. Because real love isn't about loving someone only when everything is perfect. Real love is choosing them even when things become difficult. It's understanding them when they cannot explain themselves. It's giving reassurance when they are afraid. It's holding them when words aren't enough. Because we are all imperfect. We all have our good days and our bad days. And maybe that's what love really means... Not finding someone perfect, but finding someone whose imperfections you are willing to understand. Someone you can choose again and again. Someone you can look at and say, "I know you're not perfect. Neither am I. But I still choose you." Maybe that's what loving someone for eternity means. To love the whole person. The good. The bad. The ugly. And everything in between. And if I ever get another chance to love you... Maybe next time, I will love you right.
